
# Skillmark — Training Program Manager

## Project Overview

Skillmark is a platform designed to help trainers and small organizations create and manage training programs from start to finish.

Instead of focusing on complex organization management and permissions, the platform focuses on the core training experience:

* Creating training programs.
* Adding educational content.
* Enrolling learners.
* Tracking progress.
* Evaluating learners through quizzes.
* Issuing digital certificates after completion.

---

## How It Works

An administrator creates a new training program, such as:

* Full Stack Development Bootcamp.
* AI Training Program.
* Employee Onboarding Course.

The administrator can add learning materials, including:

* Videos.
* PDF documents.
* Articles.
* External resources.

Learners can join available programs, follow the content, complete quizzes, and track their progress.

After completing the required content and achieving the required score, learners receive a digital certificate that can be shared and verified using a QR Code.

# Users

## Admin

The person responsible for managing the platform.

Can:

* Create and manage training programs.
* Add educational content.
* Create quizzes.
* Monitor learners.
* Issue certificates.

---

## Student

The learner who uses the platform.

Can:

* Browse available programs.
* Enroll in training programs.
* Access learning materials.
* Complete quizzes.
* Receive certificates.

---

# Core Features

## Training Program Management

Create and manage training programs containing:

* Program description.
* Learning resources.
* Quizzes.
* Completion requirements.

---

## Learner Progress Tracking

Monitor:

* Enrolled learners.
* Completion progress.
* Training status.

---

## Quiz System

Create assessments to evaluate learner understanding.

Features:

* Multiple-choice questions.
* Automatic scoring.
* Pass/fail evaluation.

---

## Digital Certificates

After completing a program:

* A digital certificate is generated.
* The certificate can be downloaded as a PDF.
* Each certificate contains a QR Code for verification.

---

## Certificate Verification

Anyone can scan the QR Code to verify:

* Certificate owner.
* Training program name.
* Issue date.
* Certificate validity.

---

# Project Goal

Build a simple and professional training management platform that helps manage the learning process and provides verifiable digital certificates.

The project focuses on the essential training workflow without the unnecessary complexity of enterprise systems such as multi-organization management, advanced permissions, and complicated role systems.
